How normalisation works

Each comparison group stores a conversion factor from every allowed unit to the group's base unit:

UnitFactor to kg₱75.00 becomes
Kilogram (KG)1.0000₱75.00 / kg
Gram (G)0.0010₱0.075 / g
Sack (SACK)25.0000₱1,875.00 / sack
The ranking is one JOIN and one ORDER BY: price / factor_to_base. Nothing is hard-coded per vendor.
