Order status

The single lifecycle authority. Every transition guard reads Order.status. Values: PENDING_PAYMENT, CONFIRMED, PREPARING, READY_FOR_PICKUP, OUT_FOR_DELIVERY, COMPLETED, CANCELLED.

Payment status

Tracks settlement only, on its own row. Taking cash does not move the order. Values: PENDING, VERIFIED, FAILED, REFUNDED.

Delivery status

Tracks courier progress separately. A delivery order reaches COMPLETED only through this path. Values: BOOKED, IN_TRANSIT, DELIVERED, FAILED, CANCELLED.
